Dating, without the parenting part.

OnePlusOne was created with a simple idea: people without children deserve a dating space designed with their circumstances in mind.

Whether you have chosen not to have children or simply have not reached that chapter of your life, OnePlusOne offers a place to meet others who understand where you are today.

No complicated formulas. No unnecessary distractions. Just people getting to know people.

People first. Possibilities always.

Having no children is what brings our community together. It is not what defines us.

One person. Another person. A possibility. That is OnePlusOne.
