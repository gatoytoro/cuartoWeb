## Who can join?

OnePlusOne is for adults 18 or older who do not have living biological children and are not parents of living children adopted as their own outside a family caregiving arrangement. A living biological child counts regardless of age, custody, residence or level of contact.

People whose children have died are welcome. They do not have to disclose their loss to other members. Caring for or legally adopting a sibling, niece, nephew or another relative does not automatically disqualify someone.

Members may or may not want children in the future. This does not affect eligibility. All members must represent their circumstances truthfully.

The confirmation in registration reads:

> I confirm that I meet OnePlusOne's no-children membership requirements, including having no living biological children or living children adopted as my own, except for permitted family caregiving arrangements.

## Canceling a subscription

To cancel a recurring OnePlusOne subscription, open your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings and select OnePlusOne. Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions. Canceling a subscription does not itself delete your OnePlusOne account.

## Deleting your account

**IMPORTANT:** Deleting your OnePlusOne account does not cancel an active subscription or stop billing. You must cancel separately through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

Any First Light or Early Risers benefit is permanently forfeited.
