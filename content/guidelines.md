A welcoming space starts with mutual respect.

1. Meet our membership requirements. Be truthful about eligibility.
2. Be authentic. Use accurate age, photos and profile details.
3. Respect boundaries. No harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse or unwanted sexual content.
4. Only adults may appear in profile photos. No images featuring children, nudity or images used without permission.
5. Protect privacy. Do not share another member's private information or conversations without consent.
6. No scams, impersonation, financial manipulation or deceptive solicitation.
7. Report concerns through in-app reporting and block tools. Reports are reviewed; violations may lead to restrictions or removal.
8. Treat people as whole individuals. Different future family intentions are welcome.

Come as you are. Treat others well. Let connections happen naturally.
