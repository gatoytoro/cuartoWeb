## Mission

To make meaningful dating simpler for adults without children.

We create a welcoming space where adults without children can meet, connect and build relationships without uncertainty about whether a potential match is already a parent.

You do not have to explain that part of your life before a conversation can start.

### Common ground, not a label

Having no children is what brings our community together. It is not what defines us.

### Every future is welcome

Some members never want children; others may want them in the future. Our community welcomes both.

### A different starting point

OnePlusOne is not anti-parent or anti-family. It is simply a different starting point for dating.

## Membership

The earlier you arrive, the warmer the welcome.

Places are counted in each launch city. The first members get the best of it, for the life of their account.

## Who can join

Adults 18 or older, without living children of their own.

OnePlusOne is for adults 18 or older who do not have living biological children and are not parents of living children adopted as their own outside a family caregiving arrangement.

The full rule is on [Help](/help#who-can-join).

## Safety

### Adults only in photos

Only adults may appear in profile photos. No images featuring children, nudity or images used without permission.

### Respect

No harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory abuse or unwanted sexual content.

### Report and block

Report concerns through in-app reporting and block tools. Reports are reviewed; violations may lead to restrictions or removal.

The full list is on the [Community Guidelines](/guidelines).
